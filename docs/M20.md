# M20: what M19 left

M19 shipped as v2.5.0 (#81). M20 finishes what it left, runs the checks it didn't, and takes the polish from M18's list. It's built as M19 was:
1. This page, with the decisions.
2. Step 0: the sources ([m20/sources.md](m20/sources.md)).
3. Streams in worktrees, started on the user's word, merged one at a time into `m20` with a merge check each.
4. A final pass alone and with two players, and the release checks.

## The decisions (the user's, 2026-11-14)

1. **Kept as shipped:**
   - hermit crabs as bait at Pearl's;
   - her challenge pay, $40 to $150;
   - the radio's 60 m margin;
   - the records board's line-class line by IGFA's ratio.
2. **The blue-water speargun's floats:** one float holds a fish to about 40 kg (35 kg after step 0, below). Extra floats are sold at Marta's, at about the real $135 each, to hold bigger ones.
3. **M19's unfinished pieces, all four:**
   - the surf's water height, where the CPU's and the drawn water disagreed;
   - an anchor's splash spooking flats fish;
   - the blue-water gun's float drawn on other players' copies, and the landing net drawn;
   - the boats' details: the Pilot less steady stood up, the console's trolling motor running on batteries, a Power-Pole not holding on rock or coral.
4. **The checks M19 didn't run, all of them:**
   - the mate with two players;
   - the full two-player sweep;
   - the load-time A/B and one screen's play on one core;
   - high's cloud shadows on the flats against the drawn clouds;
   - the piles' wet band in the sun.
5. **The polish from M18's list:**
   - **The tone curve:** three.js's Neutral curve tried beside ACES on every look and every tier, and switched to only if it's better overall.
   - **The sounds** M17 added, heard and measured in the game.
   - **A boat's wake** in shallow water.
   - **The moon in the sea**, of the moon's finer points.
6. **Not chosen, so they stay on M18's list:** chartering, seasons, the price review, and the moon's other finer points (eclipses, libration and parallax, the wildlife minding it). (2026-11-15) And a wake's waves slowing and turning as they run over a bank (refraction).

## What step 0 found, and the user's calls on it (2026-11-14)

The sources are in [m20/sources.md](m20/sources.md). What they set without a question:
- **The trolling motor:**
  - It draws 52 A at full thrust at 36 V (Minn Kota), going as thrust to the power 1.5 (Garmin's table), so 18 A at half.
  - Its bank is three 12 V deep-cycle batteries, 80 Ah usable: 1.5 h flat out, 4.3 h at half.
  - It charges at 10 A a battery, at the dock (4 to 6 h) or from the outboards' alternators under way.
- **The Power-Pole and the kayak's pole:** they hold on sand, mud and grass and won't stake on rock. On coral they're refused: the parks and the Keys' sanctuary forbid anchoring on living coral.
- **Wakes in shallow water:**
  - The wake's angle opens past Kelvin's 19.5° from a depth Froude number of about 0.6, to 90° at 1, then narrows as asin( 1 / Fh ).
  - On the plane the console's visible arms hardly change; her real shallow-water case is coming on or off the plane (7 to 8.5 knots in 1.4 to 2 m of water), and a kayak's on the flats.
  - **A plan change:** the crests keep their height until they break at about 0.78 of the depth, then foam. Today's fade from 1.5 m down is wrong by the physics.
- **The moon in the sea:**
  - The path's width follows the wind (Cox and Munk's slopes): about 4° in calm, 6 to 7° in fair weather and 7 to 9° in a breeze, with the moon 10° up.
  - **A plan change:** it's drawn from moonrise, at dusk too, not only once the moon's the key light.
- **Neutral against ACES:**
  - Neutral keeps hue and lifts the dark paints 2 to 4 times at the ×1.4 exposure that matches mid-grey.
  - The sea and the sky come out more saturated, and highlights clip sooner.

The user's calls on what clashed with the decisions or left a choice:
- **One float holds 35 kg**, inside the sources' 23 to 36 kg, not the 40 decided: two floats to 70 kg, three to 105.
- **The tone curve's trial compares ACES, Neutral and AgX.** Neutral's designer advises AgX for wide-range scenes like this one; the game turned AgX down long ago for greying the sand and the sea. Whichever's best overall is used, or ACES stays.
- **Standing in the Pilot:**
  - Past about 14° of heel you go overboard and she stays upright. Ship research has a standing person stepping or grabbing at about a quarter of their weight sideways, and a kayak test has the angler in before the kayak flips; the 14° is judgement.
  - Sitting keeps today's capsize limits.

## How it's built

**Step 0: the sources**
- the trolling motor's batteries (a 36 V bank, the draw at full thrust, run time, charging);
- the Power-Pole on hard bottom;
- a stand-up kayak's stability stood up;
- blue-water floats by the fish's size;

**Wave 1**
- **SEA**
  - **The surf:** measured with the sea's real surface (`tools/wakes/look.mjs`' match mode), the tool's clock fixed first. Then the CPU made to match: the shore wave's sideways movement undone, the roller's lumps.
  - **The moon** in the sea's reflection, from uniforms alone.
  - **Wakes** in shallow water.

  **Built (stream SEA, 2026-11-14; [m20/sea.md](m20/sea.md)).**
  - **The surf's water height.** The CPU finds the resting point the sea draws at a point, through the breakers' push too, and where a breaking face folds over it takes the highest water. Each wave's height and the roller's lumps come from an integer hash, the same to the bit on both sides; a float hash had the CPU's waves drift from the drawn ones as a day's waves counted up. `tools/checks/shore-match.mjs` sets the CPU's height against every drawn pixel. The beach's swash on mid went from 7.7 to 12.7 cm rms to 1.3 to 4.9, the bay from 1.9 to 4.6 cm to under 1 mm. Low's 2.3 m grid still draws the breakers in chords, up to 10 cm off. A height in the surf costs 25 to 30 µs, against 4 to 7.
  - **The moon in the sea,** from moonrise, its path as wide as Cox and Munk's slopes make it for the wind. Of 18 paths measured (5, 10 and 20° up; calm, fair and breezy; night and twilight), 10 fall inside their range and the rest within 25%. The key light's own glint is kept off the sea while the moon's the key. Uniforms only.
  - **Wakes in shallow water.** Her waves go as the depth under her has them. Her arms open toward the wedge over the transcritical range, and her crests stand twice as high near critical. Each crest breaks at 0.78 of the depth, white where it does, in place of the fade from 1.5 m. Coming off the plane at 8.2 kn over the bay's 2.2 m bank, her arms are 34° out, her waves 12.7 m long and her crest 0.49 m; out at sea they're 19°, 6.9 m and 0.24 m. No foam's shed in water shallower than she draws. A wave doesn't turn or slow as it runs over a bank.
  - `test/surf-height.mjs`, and `test/wakes.mjs`' shallow cases.
- **KIT**
  - **The anchor's splash:** it spooks flats fish, shared with the others.
  - **The blue-water gun:** its extra floats, and its float on copies, with the copies' gun level fixed.
  - **The landing net,** drawn.
  - **The boats:** the Pilot stood up, the trolling batteries, the Power-Pole on coral and rock.
  - **`kayaksand`.**

  **Built (stream KIT, 2026-11-14; [m20/kit.md](m20/kit.md)).**
  - **The floats:** one holds a fish to 35 kg. Extra floats are $135 each at Marta's, bought with the blue-water gun only; two hold 70 kg, three 105, four 140. A heavier fish drags them under and pulls free. In the sweep a 40 kg wahoo comes in 15% of the time on one float and every time on two.
  - **On other players' copies:** the blue-water gun (it was drawn as the railgun), its floats where the owner's ride, and the flopper open.
  - **The landing net, drawn,** on your game and your copy.
  - **The anchor's splash** spooks the flats' fish within 15 m (the Power-Poles' thump 6 m, a stake-out pole 4 m), shared as any spook is, and the Guide says why.
  - **Standing in the Pilot:** past 14° of heel you go in and she stays upright. Beam on she heels 2°, 6° and 8° in calm, fair and breezy weather.
  - **The trolling motor's batteries:** 80 Ah, 1.5 h flat out, 4.4 h at half, charged from her outboards or the dock's charger overnight. A gauge in the purse.

**Wave 2**
- **FINISH:** the tone curve's trial; the sounds measured in the game.

  **Built (stream FINISH, 2026-11-15; [m20/finish.md](m20/finish.md)).**
  - **The tone curve: ACES stays.** Neutral (at ×1.4) and AgX were drawn over every look on every tier at the exposure matching ACES' mid-grey, beside the photographs. Neutral brings barn red and navy to the photographs (two to four times ACES' levels); but it lifts the night to a saturated navy, takes the white heart out of the transom lights' pool, and turns the swash a bright cyan. AgX greys the sea and the sand, as the game found long ago. `?tone=neutral|agx` stays for another trial, on the game's own page only.
  - **The moon's path on high** no longer hazes the night. The glint's kept under the bloom's threshold, which takes the scene's light before the exposure. The sea beside the path had read 0.076 against low and mid's 0.016; it now reads 0.016 on a calm and a fair night, and the path's drawn as wide as mid's. Low and mid unchanged.
  - **The sounds, measured in the game** (`tools/audio/levels.mjs`): the tail, the bolt, the anchor's splash, Pearl idling and flat out, the kayak's push and another player's kayak, each within 1.7 dB of its level on every tier. Before:
    - the tail and the bolt were 3 to 4 dB quiet (a mono sound's 3 dB trim taken again through its panner);
    - the push at her seat was 4.8 dB quiet on low (through her panner from under you; now unpanned within 1.5 m of her);
    - Pearl was 4 to 5 dB loud on mid and high (Chromium's HRTF adds 4.4 dB to her outboard; now taken off there, `tools/audio/hrtf.mjs`);
    - **another player's kayak was never heard:** M19's state field never reached the mix. It does now, from where she is.
  - `test/flats-sounds.mjs`' Pearl on two tiers and another's kayak.
