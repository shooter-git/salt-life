# M20: what M19 left

M19 shipped as v2.5.0 (#81). M20 finishes what it left, runs the checks it didn't, and takes the polish from M18's list. It's built as M19 was:
1. This page, with the decisions.
2. Step 0: the sources ([m20/sources.md](m20/sources.md)).
3. Streams in worktrees, started on the user's word, merged one at a time into `m20` with a merge check each.
4. A final pass alone and with two players, and the release checks.

## The decisions (the user's, 2026-11-14)

1. **Kept as shipped:**
   - hermit crabs as bait at Pearl's;
   - her challenge pay, $40 to $150;
   - the radio's 60 m margin;
   - the records board's line-class line by IGFA's ratio.
2. **The blue-water speargun's floats:** one float holds a fish to about 40 kg (35 kg after step 0, below). Extra floats are sold at Marta's, at about the real $135 each, to hold bigger ones.
3. **M19's unfinished pieces, all four:**
   - the surf's water height, where the CPU's and the drawn water disagreed;
   - an anchor's splash spooking flats fish;
   - the blue-water gun's float drawn on other players' copies, and the landing net drawn;
   - the boats' details: the Pilot less steady stood up, the console's trolling motor running on batteries, a Power-Pole not holding on rock or coral.
4. **The checks M19 didn't run, all of them:**
   - the mate with two players;
   - the full two-player sweep;
   - the load-time A/B and one screen's play on one core;
   - high's cloud shadows on the flats against the drawn clouds;
   - the piles' wet band in the sun.
5. **The polish from M18's list:**
   - **The tone curve:** three.js's Neutral curve tried beside ACES on every look and every tier, and switched to only if it's better overall.
   - **The sounds** M17 added, heard and measured in the game.
   - **A boat's wake** in shallow water.
   - **The moon in the sea**, of the moon's finer points.
6. **Not chosen, so they stay on M18's list:** chartering, seasons, the price review, and the moon's other finer points (eclipses, libration and parallax, the wildlife minding it).

## What step 0 found, and the user's calls on it (2026-11-14)

The sources are in [m20/sources.md](m20/sources.md). What they set without a question:
- **The trolling motor:**
  - It draws 52 A at full thrust at 36 V (Minn Kota), going as thrust to the power 1.5 (Garmin's table), so 18 A at half.
  - Its bank is three 12 V deep-cycle batteries, 80 Ah usable: 1.5 h flat out, 4.3 h at half.
  - It charges at 10 A a battery, at the dock (4 to 6 h) or from the outboards' alternators under way.
- **The Power-Pole and the kayak's pole:** they hold on sand, mud and grass and won't stake on rock. On coral they're refused: the parks and the Keys' sanctuary forbid anchoring on living coral.
- **Wakes in shallow water:**
  - The wake's angle opens past Kelvin's 19.5° from a depth Froude number of about 0.6, to 90° at 1, then narrows as asin( 1 / Fh ).
  - On the plane the console's visible arms hardly change; her real shallow-water case is coming on or off the plane (7 to 8.5 knots in 1.4 to 2 m of water), and a kayak's on the flats.
  - **A plan change:** the crests keep their height until they break at about 0.78 of the depth, then foam. Today's fade from 1.5 m down is wrong by the physics.
- **The moon in the sea:**
  - The path's width follows the wind (Cox and Munk's slopes): about 4° in calm, 6 to 7° in fair weather and 7 to 9° in a breeze, with the moon 10° up.
  - **A plan change:** it's drawn from moonrise, at dusk too, not only once the moon's the key light.
- **Neutral against ACES:**
  - Neutral keeps hue and lifts the dark paints 2 to 4 times at the ×1.4 exposure that matches mid-grey.
  - The sea and the sky come out more saturated, and highlights clip sooner.

The user's calls on what clashed with the decisions or left a choice:
- **One float holds 35 kg**, inside the sources' 23 to 36 kg, not the 40 decided: two floats to 70 kg, three to 105.
- **The tone curve's trial compares ACES, Neutral and AgX.** Neutral's designer advises AgX for wide-range scenes like this one; the game turned AgX down long ago for greying the sand and the sea. Whichever's best overall is used, or ACES stays.
- **Standing in the Pilot:**
  - Past about 14° of heel you go overboard and she stays upright. Ship research has a standing person stepping or grabbing at about a quarter of their weight sideways, and a kayak test has the angler in before the kayak flips; the 14° is judgement.
  - Sitting keeps today's capsize limits.

## Found while planning

- **Other players see the blue-water gun as the railgun:** `Together.js` clamps a copy's gun level to 0..2.
- **M19's swash measurement didn't stop the breakers' clock.**
  - `tools/checks/water.mjs` set the game's clock back each frame, but only after `App.frame` had already run `shore.update`, so the drawn surf ran a frame ahead of the CPU's.
  - [m19/checks.md](m19/checks.md) says the clock was stopped "the same for both"; for the breakers it wasn't.
  - Part of the 6 to 12 cm may be the tool's, so it's measured again before anything's fixed.
- **`tools/two-players/resume.mjs` lacks the `kayaksand` scenario** M19 asked for.
- **The listening page (`tools/sounds/`) isn't in the repository,** so the sounds are measured in the game.

## How it's built

  - **The boats:** the Pilot stood up, the trolling batteries, the Power-Pole on coral and rock.

**Wave 2**
- **FINISH:** the tone curve's trial; the sounds measured in the game.
- **CHECKS,** last, on the merged build:
  - the mate with two players (`crew.mjs`'s `mate` part);
  - a runner for the full two-player sweep;

**Rules**
- No stream adds a texture to the sea's shader: it's at 16 of 16 on high.
- Any change to a shader in the loading frame goes through the ready A/B and `tools/gpu-limits.mjs`.
- SEA alone touches `WaterSurface.js`; KIT alone touches `Speargun.js`, `Rigs.js` and the boats' controllers.
