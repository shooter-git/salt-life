- The lines and the fight ([m17/lines.md](m17/lines.md)):

An 80-class reel's 870 m (the Tiagra 80W's) would land them to about 367 kg alone and 400 with a second player backing down; the last, to the fish table's 450 kg, need the boat chasing faster than the crew's 0.4 m/s. About a third of the blue marlin hooked can't be landed now (`docs/m17/lines.md`'s "130 lb" note is wrong on this);
  - real reels hold more line than the game's spools (180, 220, 350 and 600 m), and `spoolM` also sets the sinker's and the troll's reach, so it's its own change;
  - one line goes on every rod, so with 80 lb bought you fight a bonefish on it;
  - the sweep's player doesn't steer the boat;
  - judgement, no source: when full drag comes in, the arms' endurance (`ARMS`, `ARMS_CHAIR`), the 10 kg held by hand, `LINE_MATCH`, and 80 lb's $750; the reels' figures are from listings whose pages weren't opened.
- `BreakingSeas` counts only short seas, so a long sea's head can go through a boat without coming aboard ([m16/north.md](m16/north.md)).
