# M16: the boats, built

The boats to work up to, on M15's bigger sea ([M15.md](M15.md)). M16 was built after M15 and on it (2026-10-31 to 2026-11-02): what it took from M15 is in From M15. This page keeps what was proposed and says, under each part, what was built and where the detail is (`docs/m16/*.md`, one page for each part of the work). Where the build differs from the proposal, the text says so, and What differed gathers it. What M16 leaves is in [M17.md](M17.md).

## How it was built

Ten parallel sessions on one foundation, each in its own git worktree and branch, merged one at a time into `m16-boats`; then a second wave that needed the models (the opening, the sound); then a final pass over the whole of it, alone and with two players. The foundation is `e4a32dc` (M16's foundation: a boat is any boat): `world/boat/Vessel.js`, `Boats.js` and `Handling.js` ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), The boats). ### The decisions (the user's, 2026-10-31)

- The boats must look good and work both alone and together. Hulls are in code, with Blender (scripted, as `tools/props` and `tools/characters` are) for moulded fittings such as the outboards, the seats and the fighting chair.
- Everything in the inventory below and all of M15's leftovers, the north side's extras included. Lobster traps stay in.
- Those playing now keep the lobster boat, are given the kayak, and can buy the center console from Marta. New players start in the kayak. Any boat you own can be taken out. A boat still out at sea comes home for Marta's tow fee when you switch to another.
- Low's script memory stays at 150 MB unless a real 2 GB device passes.
- The way over to the north beach is made easy to find.

## From M15

What M16 needs M15 ([M15.md](M15.md)) to have built first:

- The floating origin, the offshore seabed and the far water: without them the sportfisher has nowhere to go and the center console's near offshore is a flat empty sea.
- The offshore features (weedlines, debris, birds): what the tower and the polarized glasses are for.
- The far water's kinds of water for the bites, with the mahi and tuna out in them: M16's far fish and tackle go into them.
- Navigation, built for the lobster boat: M16 sells it for each boat.
- Picking up where you left off and the anchored-boat rules: M16's boats each use them, and its deep-water anchor and drift sock are what let one lie at anchor offshore.
- The catch keeping so long, and the tow priced by the distance: M16's ice, stores, flares and EPIRB build on them.
- Beachcombing and its buyers.

All of that was there. M15's built (2026-10-30). What it left, for consideration here with the rest, and what became of each:

- **Room first, built.** All the Spaces' checks pass, but script memory's peaked at 150 of the low tier's 150 MB at a full-speed check (113 to 121 on fewer cores) and the whole download at 75 of 80 MB, and draw calls reach about 110 of 150 in play: more boats need room found. Measuring a real 2 GB phone would say whether the low tier's 150 should be higher (`packages/qa/src/budgets.ts`, which keeps no device out). The cold load's about 6 s where 7 failed the server. Before any release: the complete check on a clean copy, and A/B the ready time ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), Testing).
  - **Room found (2026-11-01):** script memory's now 125.2 MB where v1.11.1 read 160.1, a complete check of each one after the other on the same machine (a third read 137.0 with the machine busier; peaks swing 20 MB): about 20 MB less held once it's all built, the rest garbage that no longer piles up. What did it: the masks and a field's depths let go, the plants' records and the village's shapes after the build, a trader's clips in arrays of their own, the models decoded in a worker, more of what's only drawn off the CPU, 5 of a plant's 16 matrix numbers, no character past Latin-1 in the script (`core/Glyphs.js`, 3.4 MB: one doubles it), working arrays detached rather than dropped (`core/FreeNow.js`), and two loops without iterators. `SETTLED` came down with it (75 MB, 90 before Joe and Marta) so the late stages come when they did. Left: 1 MB in the detail texture's copy (its source is hashed for the bake), 3.6 MB of the boat's arrays (the new boats read them), the reef's 10 MB, and about 30 MB of garbage as Joe and Marta load.
  - **The boats went in under it.** The streams' own readings, each on the complete check or in a driven game: 136.2 MB of script memory with the fish ([fish.md](m16/fish.md), Budgets), 129 MB alone with the together stream's code ([together.md](m16/together.md), Readings). The boat you start in is smaller in memory than the lobster boat was: the kayak's geometry is 0.46 MB against the lobster boat's 2.89 MB, before her rider's figure ([kayak.md](m16/kayak.md), Budget). The complete check on the merged tip, on a clean copy, and the ready-time A/B are the release's, as they are for every release.
  - **Low's 150 MB stays.** Nothing here measured a real 2 GB device, which is what it takes to justify more ([M17.md](M17.md)).
- **The sea's draw, built (the phone's frame rate not).** It was half as much again as v1.10.1's on the CPU's GPU, and a phone's frame rate hasn't been read since M15's early steps (`?readout`). The whitecaps were weighed and cut: calm blue water 8.7% off, fair by the dock 8.0% off, on the CPU's GPU with a phone's shader. A gale's sea was then given its look back and is 14% over v1.11.1's draw with all of it on ([north.md](m16/north.md), A gale's seas, and the sea's draw). No phone's frame rate has been read since.
- **The plotter, built.** The far sheet's blur over the bank is a vector chart close in, the night colours are S-52's, the Hump's label is over its top, the chart has proper names, the compass card rides level on its gimbal, and the radar reads magnetic as the plotter does ([north.md](m16/north.md), The plotter and the compass).
- **The radio, built.** The set's hiss and squelch are recordings; Marta brings out a can of diesel in place of a tow; Pearl comes out for you on the north side ([north.md](m16/north.md), Pearl's day, and help on the water).
- **Far out with others: boats seen as a speck past the fog, built; two real devices through All the Spaces, not.** The specks are `together/Specks.js` ([together.md](m16/together.md), Past the fog). The two-player tools (`tools/two-players/`) only stand in for the real thing, and still do.
- **The north side, built.** The turtles' sounds and hatchlings and their minding of lights and people, finds on other shores and a shell collection, Pearl's boat coming and going, lights at the camp at night, and its roof taking a hurricane ([north.md](m16/north.md)).
- **A gale's seas, built.** They were drawn 5 to 7 ft, where a real one's would be about twice that. They are 12 ft in a gale and about 18 past the far grounds, on longer waves running down the wind ([north.md](m16/north.md), A gale's seas, and the sea's draw).

Asked for by the user after M15, and built in the same stream: the way over to the north beach made easy to find, the whole chart on the Fish Hold's Chart tab, and the way home in the HUD's panel ([north.md](m16/north.md)).

## Boats to work up to

You start in a small boat suited to the island's shallows, and buy your way up to boats that reach new water, new fish and new ways to fish. Each one is walked about, every deck of it.

**Built.** All four are in, each its own `Vessel` with its own `handling`; the three new ones are modelled after photographs and published figures of a real boat of her kind (the sources are in each page). Only the boat you're in is built; the others you own lie at their berths as a few hundred triangles. The lengths are the code's (`Boats.js`, the lines files): the kayak 12 ft (3.66 m), the center console 23 ft 5 in (7.14 m), the lobster boat 27 ft (8.2 m) and the sportfisher 48 ft 9 in (14.85 m).

1. **A fishing kayak** (about 12 ft, paddled): the start. No fuel, no cost to run. The flats and the sheltered shallows by the village, sight-fishing for redfish, snook and trout. A small cooler. Low to the water, so a squall's a real danger.
   - **Built** ([kayak.md](m16/kayak.md)), after a Perception Pescador 12.0: paddled with a stroke that sets her speed, sat in (the camera is your eyes, 0.81 m off the water), a 12 kg cooler, no fuel. In a squall she goes over: past 28 to 41 degrees of heel, by what you're doing, she turns keel up, you're in the water, part of the cooler goes, and you swim to her and climb back. You fish from the seat, and a fish tows her. She lies on the sand by the pier, not at the dock. The radio and flares work from the seat, and she has a compass on the HUD.
   - **Not built: the sight-fishing.** Redfish, snook and trout are on the flats, in the creek and in the bay, and you catch them as everything else here. The polarized glasses show fish under the far water's weed, debris and birds, not on the flats. See [M17.md](M17.md).
2. **A center console** (about 23 ft, outboards, a T-top): the first engine, and fuel to think about. Fast, and the first boat to reach the reef, the wrecks and the near offshore water. The deck goes all the way round the helm, so you fish from the bow or the stern. A ladder at the stern and room for tanks: diving and spearfishing on the reef start here (the speargun has a boat for it). No cabin: a squall soaks you, and a blow sends you running in.
   - **Built** ([console.md](m16/console.md)), after a Regulator 23: a deep-V with the maker's lines measured off its plan, outboards that steer by the direction of their thrust, a T-top with drop curtains that come down in rain, a walkway round the console, a door through the transom to the bracket, a folding dive ladder and four tanks racked behind the leaning post. A downpour soaks you in about 18 seconds if you're out from under the T-top. Gasoline.
3. **The lobster boat** (27 ft, diesel, a wheelhouse): the boat we have now, its stern open to stand and fish from. The first real shelter from the weather, a big hold, nearshore and the reef's edge. Lobstering comes with it (its hauler and davit are aboard already; the traps taken off the deck in M14 are in git at 6bfc0d9 or before, `DeckGear.buildTraps`, `BoatMaterials.createTrap`, the trap colliders in `BoatModel`).
   - **Built, and the length corrected.** The proposal said 36 ft. She is 8.2 m, 27 ft: `Boats.js` says `feet: 27`, `BoatModel` is "~8.2 m", and her lines run from z -3.9 to 4.3. You can walk to her transom and fish from it with eight traps stowed on her quarters (`tools/two-players/seen.mjs stern` walks it); a guest with traps of his own was walking into their colliders on her copy, which was mended (`BoatModel.walkedAs`).
   - **The traps were rebuilt, not restored from git.** They are the Caribbean kind, rusty chicken wire on a frame of bleached poles after photographs from Barbados, set from the hauler, soaked over real days, hauled with the davit, and in the group's save as buoys everyone sees ([traps.md](m16/traps.md)).
4. **An offshore sportfisher** (about 48 ft, twin diesels): past the reef for the bluewater fish (mahi, tuna, sailfish, marlin). Up in its tuna tower you look for birds working bait, weedlines, debris and colour in the water, and fish on top, through polarized glasses into the water. The captain steers from the flybridge below it and watches the baits. When a rod goes off you go down to the cockpit, aft, to fight it from the chair. Big and fast enough to run ahead of a storm. (After a hurricane, the mahi gather under its debris offshore.)
   - **Built** ([sportfisher.md](m16/sportfisher.md)), after the Viking 48 and the Hatteras 48, 48 ft 9 in: a Carolina bow, a cockpit with a mezzanine, a house with a salon, a galley, a dinette, a companionway, a bunk room, a head and a stateroom, a flybridge with a centre helm, a hardtop, and a tuna tower whose platform stands at 7 m with a standing eye at 8.62 m. Up to the bridge by a ladder from the mezzanine and on to the tower by a long one: a real climb, in which a rolling boat throws you sideways and your grip drains (`LadderClimb`). With the tower fitted she has a second helm up there. Twin diesels, 18.5 t, 32.7 knots flat out, on the plane at a cruising lever. On a phone she is 13 draws, with the cabin, wheel, radar, chair and displays left out of view past 70 m.
   - The mahi gather under a hurricane's debris (`Finds.hurricaneWake`, [fish.md](m16/fish.md)).

The sportfisher has to be worth saving up for. What it gives you:

- Days out: bunks, a galley, a fish box with ice, and fuel for the far grounds. Real ones make overnight runs to the deep water as a matter of course. Sleep aboard to pass the night; the catch keeps as long as the ice does; fuel and stores to watch; a big hold to fill before the run home.
  - **Built** ([days-out.md](m16/days-out.md)): bunks (E beside one sleeps till dawn or dusk), her fish box and an icemaker with a generator, stores for 14 days, a hold of 300 kg and, with the big fish box, 600.
- Fish of its own, because mahi and tuna are in the fish table already and it needs more than those to be worth it:
  - wahoo, fast, caught trolling;
  - sailfish, white marlin and blue marlin, mostly let go, the island's trophies;
  - swordfish at night, or deep-dropped by day: the reason to stay out overnight;
  - bigger yellowfin and blackfin tuna, and the mahi moved mostly offshore.
  - **Built** ([fish.md](m16/fish.md)): all of them, each with its published length-weight relation, a model and skin after photographs, and its place in the log and the orders. The billfish are never kept: the release bonus pays for them and the log keeps the best of each as a trophy (`Trophies.js`).
- Ways to fish of its own:
  - trolling a spread of baits off the outriggers;
  - kite fishing for sailfish;
  - deep-dropping on an electric reel.
  - **Built** ([tackle.md](m16/tackle.md)): all three, and a sinker for the bottom, chum, and a real drag with a spool, so a fish far over the line can be fought.
- Every part of it to go about: climbing the tower's ladder (a climb of its own: the pier's walkable stair boxes won't do for something that high and steep, nor for falling off it in a seaway), the cabin and galley inside, the flybridge's helm, and the fighting chair.
  - **Built:** every part of it ([sportfisher.md](m16/sportfisher.md), Going about her).

Earned, not only bought: the sportfisher is in Marta's list from the start, but as a silhouette, with its name and no picture, and "finish your upgrades to unlock it" under it, with how far you are (12 of 19 upgrades, say). It's unlocked, and its picture shown, once you've:

- every upgrade on the lobster boat, the boat before it: the hold, tank, engine, fish finder, floodlights, anchor, bilge pump and livewell (12 upgrades, $3,050 from new);
- your rod and reel gear at its best: rod, reel and line (7 upgrades, $1,435 from new).

That is, mastery of the boat you're on and of the fishing the sportfisher's built for. The speargun, the cast net and the tickle stick aren't asked for: someone who only fishes with a rod shouldn't have to buy diving and lobstering gear to get there. Then it still has to be paid for.

- **Built as proposed** (`Gear.UNLOCK`, `unlockProgress`; [fleet.md](m16/fleet.md), Marta's list): 12 and 7 make 19, as the doc counted them. A level past those (the big-game rod and reel) doesn't count, nor do the traps. Once earned she stays earned, so selling the lobster boat doesn't lock her again.

A trawler was weighed for the last one and set aside: net fishing would be a whole new way to fish for one boat, where the sportfisher builds on the rod and reel. A poled flats skiff was weighed for the second and set aside: it fished the kayak's water the kayak's way, only faster.

What it would take, and what became of each:

- The three new boats modelled from photographs of their kinds (side, stern, deck and tower views gathered first), and each its own handling: a paddled kayak is a different control altogether. **Built.** The handling a boat carries is in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), The boats.
- The save: the boats you own and the one you're in. Those playing now keep the lobster boat: none of them goes back to a kayak. **Built.** The save is version 2 (`boat`, `fleet`, `earned`, `dues`); a version 1 save loads as the lobster boat with her gear and tank, and the kayak beside her.
- Playing together: the others' boats, each the boat it is, on your sea. **Built** ([together.md](m16/together.md)).
- Somewhere in the village to buy them (Marta's?), and their prices against what the fish fetch. **Built:** Marta's Boats tab; the prices are tuned in [opening.md](m16/opening.md).
- What each one can stand: the weather and how far out, which is most of the design. **Built** ([days-out.md](m16/days-out.md), What each boat can stand).
- Picking up where you left off: built in M15 for the lobster boat; each new boat uses it. **Built:** each boat's away record, her berth, her anchor and sock, and a hurricane at her berth; `tools/two-players/resume.mjs` and `resume-together.mjs` load the page for real in each.
- The lobster boat's stern: in `BoatModel` its deck already runs to the transom, so whatever keeps you off it is to be found (a leftover trap collider, the bait barrel, or where you're let walk). **Found and mended** for a guest on her copy (the stowed traps' colliders); see above.

## Per-boat upgrades

The upgrades now (`Gear.js`) are of two kinds:

- Your own gear, which goes with you onto any boat: the rod, reel, line, speargun, cast net and tickle stick.
- The boat's gear, which belongs to its hull: the hold, tank, engine, fish finder, floodlights, anchor and bilge pump, and the livewell most likely.

Each boat gets its own boat upgrades, sized to it:

- the kayak: a crate, a better paddle, an anchor trolley, a fish finder puck;
- the center console: bigger outboards, the T-top, a livewell, the dive ladder;
- the lobster boat: what it has now;
- the sportfisher: the outriggers, the tower, an electric reel, a bigger fish box, an icemaker, a generator.

A boat's upgrades stay with it when you buy the next one, and trading it in gets some of their worth back.

**Built**, with the prices in The full inventory. The kayak also has a dry hatch (half as much of the cooler goes when she turns over), where the proposal said "a life jacket's worth of room"; the console a tank rack; the lobster boat lobster traps; the sportfisher a fighting chair and underwater lights. Each upgrade is a track in `UPGRADES` with an `on` naming the boat, so it counts only while she's the boat you're in and stays with her when you're in another. Trading in or selling a boat resets her tracks ([fleet.md](m16/fleet.md), Buying, trading in, selling).

## The rest of it

- Money: the upgrades cost 45 to 700 now, the boats need thousands. The new offshore fish's prices and what the big boats cost to run (the sportfisher burns far more fuel, plus its upkeep and a slip at the dock) tuned together, so each boat takes a good while to earn but isn't a grind.
  - **Built.** The upgrades now run from $30 to the tower's $4,000, and the boats cost $1,800, $6,000 and $25,000. `tools/earnings.mjs` plays a steady hand's hour at each stage: the whole climb is about 15 hours of steady play, reckoned at about 30 for a real player ([opening.md](m16/opening.md), The prices). The slip and upkeep are small beside what a boat earns; opening.md says why they were left that way. The prices want a real player to judge them.
- The sportfisher's crew: riding along exists already. A big boat gives a group real jobs at once: one at the helm, one up the tower spotting, one in the chair. Perhaps the best reason to own one.
  - **Built** (`together/Crew.js`): you take a job by going where it's done, the helm, the tower (calling what you see by the clock from her bow) or the chair, and those aboard are told.
- Starting in a kayak: those playing now keep the lobster boat and its upgrades. New players start in the kayak: the opening and the Guide's tips rewritten for it, and Marta's haul-out before a hurricane working for every boat.
  - **Built** ([opening.md](m16/opening.md)). The yard in Marta's has a spot for each boat; a boat you're not in can be hauled out and put back from her row.
- Your fleet: where the boats you own are kept, choosing which one you take out, and selling one.
  - **Built** ([fleet.md](m16/fleet.md)): a berth for each (the console west of the pier head, the lobster boat east of it, the sportfisher along its seaward face, the kayak on the sand), E beside one to take her out, and selling and trading in.
- Phones: the sportfisher, with its cabin inside and its tower, will be the heaviest model in the game, and needs a lighter version for them.
  - **Built:** `new SportfisherModel( 'low' )` (13 draws, the cabin and the rest left out past 70 m). Her cabin and all her decks are still there.
- The billfish: let go, as the rules say. A bonus for releasing one, with its picture and the log as the trophy.
  - **Built** (`Trophies.js`): the bonus is larger with the dehooker and tag kit, and the log has a tab of the trophies let go.

## What differed

The build matches the proposal but for these:

- **The lobster boat is 27 ft, not 36 ft.** The proposal's figure doesn't match the model, which is 8.2 m (`BoatModel`, `Boats.js`).
- **The wire leader is for wahoo and barracuda**, not wahoo and kings. The fish table has no kingfish.
- **The lobster traps are new, not restored from git.** The old model at 6bfc0d9 is not in the game.
- **The center console costs $1,800**, not about $2,500, so the first engine takes about as long to earn as the next boat does. Her upkeep follows (a tenth of her price a year, $15 a month).
- **The kayak's last track is a dry hatch**, not "a life jacket's worth of room".
- **The kayak's sight-fishing is not built.**
- **The tower has a second helm.** The proposal had the captain on the flybridge; with the tower fitted you can also drive from its platform.
