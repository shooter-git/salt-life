# M9: weather, for consideration

M8 brought the weather's core ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#the-weather)): the day's wind, sea, surf, cloud and light, the boat in the wind and Marta's tow. Everything else talked about for the weather is here, to be weighed and chosen from. None of it is promised.

## Rain

- Rain around the camera: streaks in one or two draws, kept thin on low (phones' fill rate is what rain costs).
- Curtains of rain in the distance (high).
- Rings and ripples where it hits the water.
- Drips.
- Rain haze.
- Wet surfaces: one shared value, like `G.night`, darkening and glossing the ground, the village, the boat and the debris.
- Recordings (CC0, in `credits/audio.md`): rain on the water, on wood, on tin roofs and on the boat's deck. It should sound different under a roof, as footsteps do on each surface.
- The low tier's voices: a rain bed beside the surf within 14 sounds at once.

## Thunder and lightning

- A flash lighting the clouds from inside: in the cloud march on mid and high, the panorama tinted on low.
- A branching bolt, in one draw.
- Thunder delayed by its distance at 343 m/s (count from the flash), from recordings of a near crack and a far rumble.
- Strikes from a seed the forecast gives, so everyone sees the same one.
- Lightning striking the sea near a boat.

## Storm skies and the wind

- Fronts that drift in, through the clouds' weather map.
- Storm skies of their own: stratus, nimbostratus, cumulonimbus.
- The cirrus, the clouds' horizon coverage and the environment's level under a grey sky (`cirrusAmount`, `scsHorizon`, `levels.env`).
- The fog greying under cloud, with a band at the sky's horizon to match. The sky's horizon stays the clear atmosphere's, so a grey fog alone would leave a seam (`SKY_FRAGMENT`, its view and environment variants).
- The wind veering. It needs:
  - the waves' phase re-anchored around the camera as it turns (`phase += k (d_old − d_new) · p_ref`);
  - the sand's ripples and the grass combed along a fixed prevailing wind (`TerrainSurface.js`);
  - the cirrus's angle as a uniform (it's compiled in, `Clouds.js`).
- Gusts in the boat's physics, slower than 8 s (her roll is 3.3 s) and the same gusts the plants and the sea show. The wind's sound gusting with them.
- The caustics and the underwater light dimming under cloud (`Terrain.js`, `Underwater.js`, the underwater fog in `App.js`).

## The sea and the surf

- The surf's period: a long groundswell, a short wind swell. It needs a phase accumulated on the GPU, in the CPU's mirror and in the surf's sound.
- The swell from another direction: two or three shore fields baked ahead.
- The open sea's crests breaking, and spray torn off them.
- Spray from whitecaps and breakers (Tidewater's emitters on the GPU weren't ported).
- The sea's culling box following the sea state (`water.lod.maxY`), if a bigger sea ever needs it.
- `Fish.breakDepth` following the surf (it's 0.42 m).
- A surf report, or a forecast on a board.

## Consequences (mild: a cost, never a game over)

- The hull:
  - damage from slams (`BoatSpray`'s `onSlam`) and from the ground and the piles (`BoatController.contacts`);
  - water coming in, the bilge and bailing it;
  - the engine stalling;
  - repairs at Marta's.
- A catch lost, gear lost, lines snapping more often in a wind.
- The wind on fishing: how far a cast goes, the bobber's drift, the cast net's throw.
- Big surf: rip currents, being tumbled.
- Murky water for spearfishing after a blow.
- The village: shutters banging, debris.

## Fishing and the island

- Bites by the weather: a `weather` key in `Game.habitat()`, as `flood` is. Fish feed hard ahead of a front.
- Which fish are about in which weather.
- The surf changing the bait that runs inside the breakers (`runnerMullet`, `runnerFry`).
- Joe and Marta shutting in a gale.
- The forecast chalked on a slate at Joe's (as `WantedBoard`).

- The palms bent as far as they go.
