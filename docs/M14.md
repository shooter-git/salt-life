# M14: the weather and the sea, for consideration

M8 brought the weather's core, M9 its rain and thunderstorms, M10 what it costs, M11 the rain where you stand, M12 storms from other directions, and M13 the hurricane ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#the-hurricane)): a Category 1 about every forty days, the same for everyone, its surge and surf, its wind and rain in metres seen, the warnings, Marta's haul-out and the shelter, the wildlife in the weather, the roofs, the pier and the palms torn and mended, its wrack up the beach, and the eye's sky. Everything else talked about is here, to be weighed and chosen from. None of it is promised.

## More of the hurricane

What M13 turned up, or left out:

- Torn roof sheets that fly: a sheet's gone from its roof the moment its wind is reached, with nothing in the air (the flying debris is fronds, leaves and twigs, `FlyingDebris`). A sheet cartwheeling away downwind, landing where the wrack is, and carried off with it.
- The eyewall's look: a real one is a smooth, sloping stadium, and the clouds' shapes make it puffy cumulus (`eyeCover` sets only how much cloud there is: its shape is the volume's noise). A smoother erosion inside the wall, or its own shape there.
- The bands coming in: the hurricanes come down the trades, from behind the mountain as seen from the village and the beach, so a band's towers are seen coming only from the boat or the north shore.
- The low tier's panorama: it's made over seconds and draws the clouds where they were, so the eye's walls, moving at the storm's speed and racing round with its wind, may smear there. Not yet seen on a phone.
- Weed and sand over the boardwalk: washed over the paths, the boardwalk and the pier's foot, but hard to see on the boardwalk's weathered boards.
- More of the island torn: Joe's stand and Marta's table, the stilt huts' thatch, the stalls' awnings, the drying nets, Marta's dock (only the metal roofs, the pier's walkway and the plants are now).
- The shelter's sound: the shutters rattling, the radio reading the advisories.
- A stronger one, now and then: a Category 2 or 3 one in so many (more of the roofs, the sea into the village). The island's built for a Category 1 now (`DAMAGE`'s thresholds, the surge's 1.6 m at most).
- The hurricane's wall in the checks: they pick one at dusk and rest to night (in its rain, three hours before its eye) and to dawn (its damage and wrack), but the rests can't reach its wall, half an hour before its eye (`qa/script.json`).

## Storms from other directions

- The clouds aloft turning with a front: since M13 their drift and the cirrus's frame are given to them (`SkyProClouds.drift`, `upper`), and a hurricane sets them to its wind and its outflow. A front's westerly behind it could set them the same way (they keep to the trades' otherwise).
- A trades gale's own sea on the north shore: the bay's surf in a blow down the trades is the island's swell from the south grown bigger, as it always was, while the gale's sea would break on the north shore, in the lee of the mountain from the village.
- A third swell field, from the north (behind a front) or the south-west, if two crossing trains ever look wrong where a swell's between them (`SWELL_WEST` and the island's own are the two now; a hurricane's surge solves the island's own again at its height).
- Each swell's train has its own period, but the open sea's swell is one set of waves: a westerly's turns its shorter swell waves to the west (`Waves.setSwellWest`), with the same period as the rest.

## Rain

- The rain on a tin roof heard from beneath it: under the boathouse, Joe's roof, the stilt huts and the shelter the rain's muffled now, with no drumming over you (none of the recordings auditioned for M11 was kept).
- Dry under a rock's overhang: the cover map reaches only the village and the pier, and no rock standing out of the sea is in it (the splashes on the rocks about you are their own, `Rain.setRocks`).

## Thunder and lightning

- Lightning striking the sea near a boat (and the boat's crew feeling it). The strikes can't aim at a boat, and near ones are rare: it would need the host to place them.
- Several flashes at once: the sky draws the brightest one's glow, with all their light; on the horizon in a line of storms (one a second, or so) two may overlap.

## Storm skies

- Cumulonimbus: towers of their own in a thunderstorm (rain's deck is nimbostratus now: lower, smoother, darker). Since M13 a hurricane's wall and bands take cover past 1, so their tops rise higher (`scCoverOf`, up to 1.45): a thunderstorm's cell could do the same where it is. Raymarching in `SkyProClouds` at a cost a GPU would have to show. Since M12 a thunderstorm on the horizon shows a tower only while its lightning lights it (Sky's flash: a lumpy glow up to its top); by day, or between flashes, you see only its rain's curtain, under whatever cloud's there.

## The sea and the surf

- The open sea's crests breaking, and spray torn off them (since M13 a hurricane's wind tears spray off the sea about you, `StormSpray`, and streaks it with foam; its crests don't break).
- Spray from whitecaps and breakers on an ordinary day (Tidewater's emitters on the GPU weren't ported).
- The sea's culling box following the sea state (`water.lod.maxY`), if a bigger sea ever needs it (since M13 it moves and opens with the surge).

## Consequences (mild: a cost, never a game over)

- Being knocked over wading in a big surf (a swimmer's tumbled since M12, and a hurricane's wind pushes you since M13; wading, the waves don't).
- Gear lost: a snapped line's float and hook (tackle isn't an item, and charging for every snap would change a fair day).
- The village: shutters that swing and bang where you see them (they're merged into the houses: only their sound bangs now, and for a hurricane they're shut), and loose things blown about (the beach's debris is merged and still).

## The boat's instruments

- The radar: her open array turns, but no screen shows it (the centre screen is the sounder). The island's coast and the other players' boats on a third screen, or a split one; a hurricane's bands coming in on it.

## The checks

- Their frame time isn't judged: on their machine `pacedFrame` holds the frames to the display's, and the SDK's `perf` messages don't reach the report (All the Spaces' side).
- The low tier's sounds in a hurricane: 14 playing at once of the 16 allowed (9 in a thunderstorm before M13), its wind, surf and rain beds and the rest. Another bed for it would want one of them left out on low, as the rain's weakest are.
- A westerly's swell isn't in them: they start on a fair day and pick none, so its field is never solved there (a few tenths of a second in a worker, and a 4 MB float texture once it is). Since M13 they pick a hurricane, whose surge field is solved the same way.
