# M14: what's left over

M14 brought storm skies and the sea (the README; [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)): cumulonimbus and the eye's stadium and bands, the clouds aloft turning with a front, the open sea's crests breaking and the spray off them, the north shore's own sea and each swell at its own period, being knocked over wading, the village's shutters and litter in a blow, the radar, the rain heard on tin and the shelter's radio, a Category 2 or 3 now and then, more of the island torn and its roof sheets flying, and shelter under the rocks (three overhangs on the shore). What it was weighed against and left, and what it turned up, is here for the next milestone's choosing. None of it is promised.

## Left from M14's list

- Lightning striking the sea near a boat (and the boat's crew feeling it): left out of M14 by choice. The strikes can't aim at a boat, and near ones are rare: it would need the host to place them.
- Weed and sand over the boardwalk: a hurricane's wrack is washed over the paths and the pier's foot, but it's hard to see on the boardwalk's weathered boards.
- A third swell field, from the north (behind a front) or the south-west, if two crossing trains ever look wrong where a swell's between them.
- The sea's culling box following the sea state (`water.lod.maxY`), if a bigger sea ever needs it (it moves and opens with a hurricane's surge).
- Gear lost: a snapped line's float and hook (tackle isn't an item, and charging for every snap would change a fair day).

## Turned up in M14

- The rocks within about 60 m are drawn since M14 (their near shape kept none of its pixels before): their triangles on low are measured (at most 22k, by the overhangs, of frames of under 80k; none where the checks' frames peak), what they cost a phone in frame time isn't.
- Sounds left for a recording you'd pick: a roof sheet tearing off and landing.
- The low tier's one program for the village's opaque materials reads 16 textures, a phone's most: anything more for the village on low needs one of them out (the rocks' cover was kept out of it).
- Not seen on a graphics card or a phone, or in play: M14's checks were renders drawn on the CPU.

## The checks

- Their frame time isn't judged: on their machine `pacedFrame` holds the frames to the display's, and the SDK's `perf` messages don't reach the report (All the Spaces' side).
- The low tier's sounds in a hurricane: 15 playing at once of the 16 allowed (14 before M14). Another bed for it would want one of them left out on low, as the rain's weakest are.
- The low tier's script memory: 142.7 MB of the 150 allowed (122.1 before M14), and its triangles 277k of 300k: room for little more on low.
- Their play: 13½ minutes on all four screens here (the script plays 165 s of its own waits on each, and every screenshot waits for a frame drawn on the CPU), of the 25 minutes All the Spaces gives it; a busy server takes longer.
