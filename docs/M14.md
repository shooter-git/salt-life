# M14: what's left over

M14 brought storm skies and the sea (the README; [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)): cumulonimbus and the eye's stadium and bands, the clouds aloft turning with a front, the open sea's crests breaking and the spray off them, the north shore's own sea and each swell at its own period, being knocked over wading, the village's shutters and litter in a blow, the radar, the rain heard on tin and the shelter's radio, a Category 2 or 3 now and then, more of the island torn and its roof sheets flying, and shelter under the rocks (three overhangs on the shore). What it was weighed against and left, and what it turned up, is here for the next milestone's choosing. None of it is promised.

## Left from M14's list

- Lightning striking the sea near a boat (and the boat's crew feeling it): left out of M14 by choice. The strikes can't aim at a boat, and near ones are rare: it would need the host to place them.

## Turned up in M14

- The near rocks' frame time on a phone isn't measured (their triangles on low are: at most 22k, by the overhangs, of frames under 80k; none where the checks' frames peak).
- The overhangs' flow layers are grooved and banded, but still short of the photographs' stacked plates: grooves deeper than 0.22 m cut pieces off the shapes at their 0.25 m grid, so more would need a finer grid (more triangles, more time to make).
- The roof sheets' sounds (tearing, landing) are cut from the recordings chosen for them by measurement, not by ear: to be heard in play and changed on the listening page.
- The low tier's one program for the village's opaque materials reads 16 textures, a phone's most: anything more for the village on low needs one of them out (the rocks' cover was kept out of it).
- Not seen on a graphics card or a phone, or in play: M14's checks were renders drawn on the CPU.

## The checks

- Their frame time isn't judged: on their machine `pacedFrame` holds the frames to the display's, and the SDK's `perf` messages don't reach the report (All the Spaces' side).
- The sounds at once: 14 of the 16 allowed on every screen, the low tier's cap now holding as they count (a hurricane's peak had come to 16 with the sheets torn off it, counting voices still fading out).
- The low tier's script memory: at most 140 MB of the 150 allowed (122 before M14), and its triangles 277k of 300k: room for little more on low.
- Their play: 13.3 minutes on all four screens here, of the 25 minutes All the Spaces gives it; a busy server takes longer.
