# M10: weather, for consideration

M8 brought the weather's core, and M9 its rain and thunderstorms ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#the-weather)): showers and thunderstorms from the forecast, the rain around you and its rings on the water, wet surfaces, lightning (the flash, the bolts, strikes everyone sees), rain's deck and the fronts up the trades, the gusts in the boat and the wind's sound, the caustics fading under cloud, and the fish, Joe and Marta in the weather. Everything else talked about for the weather is here, to be weighed and chosen from. None of it is promised.

## Rain

- Curtains of rain in the distance (high).
- Drips (off the roofs, the wheelhouse, the palms).
- Rain splashing on the ground and the decks.
- The rain's sound on each surface: on the water, on the pier's wood, on tin roofs (the rain's beds now are a shower, a downpour and the boat's deck: `tools/audio/build-weather.mjs`). It should sound different under a roof, as footsteps do on each surface.

## Thunder and lightning

- Lightning striking the sea near a boat (and the boat's crew feeling it).
- The lightning's flash in the reflections (the environment's panorama is made over seconds; the sea doesn't show a flash).

## Storm skies and the wind

- Cumulonimbus: towers of their own in a thunderstorm (rain's deck is nimbostratus now: lower, smoother, darker).
- The environment's level under a grey sky (`levels.env`).
- The clouds aloft turning with the weather too (they keep to the trades' direction: their evolution runs along it, and the cirrus's angle is compiled in, `Clouds.js`).

## The sea and the surf

- The swell from another direction: two or three shore fields baked ahead.
- The open sea's crests breaking, and spray torn off them.
- Spray from whitecaps and breakers (Tidewater's emitters on the GPU weren't ported).
- The sea's culling box following the sea state (`water.lod.maxY`), if a bigger sea ever needs it.
- `Fish.breakDepth` following the surf (it's 0.42 m).
- Rain flattening the sea's short waves.

## Consequences (mild: a cost, never a game over)

- The hull:
  - damage from slams (`BoatSpray`'s `onSlam`) and from the ground and the piles (`BoatController.contacts`);
  - water coming in (the rain too), the bilge and bailing it;
  - the engine stalling;
  - repairs at Marta's.
- A catch lost, gear lost, lines snapping more often in a wind.
- The wind on fishing: how far a cast goes, the bobber's drift, the cast net's throw.
- Big surf: rip currents, being tumbled.
- Murky water for spearfishing after a blow.
- The village: shutters banging, debris.

## Fishing and the island

- The surf changing the bait that runs inside the breakers (`runnerMullet`, `runnerFry`).

## The hurricane

- A storm surge (`G.seaLevel` raised: the shore waves already follow the depth), and the sea over the beach.
- Surf past M8's cap, with damage to the pier and the dock.
- Rain driven sideways, and less than 100 m seen.
- Things flying.
- The palms bent as far as they go.
- Buildings damaged.
- An evacuation, as an event.

## The checks

- A weather scene of their own in `qa/script.json` (they play a fair day 1 now): a shower and a thunderstorm, for the rain's and the lightning's cost on the low tier.
