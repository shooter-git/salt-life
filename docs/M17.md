# M17: what M16 leaves, for consideration

What the boats ([M16.md](M16.md)) leave undone, found while building them and playing them, alone and with two games on one machine. Each item is still undone as far as the code and the pages show (checked 2026-11-02), or is a figure nobody has been able to settle. A few were seen in play and not traced to a line of code, and say so. The page it comes from is named where there is one. None of it is promised.

## The flats

- **Sight-fishing on the flats.** M16's proposal gave the kayak "sight-fishing for redfish, snook and trout". The polarized glasses (`game/rigs/Glasses.js`) work from the sportfisher's bridge and, far better, from her tower, but they show fish only under the far water's debris, bait and weed, and nothing on the flats is shown or stalked: redfish, snook and trout are caught as everything else is. The stalking is the piece left. Fish that notice you, and have to be stalked slowly, are the speargun's already (M7).

## Guests aboard, and at the dock

All of these are what a second player sees or can do on someone else's boat ([together.md](m16/together.md)).

**Done ahead of M17** (2026-11-05, branch `guest-deckhand`, at the user's ask: "build it all now"): a guest riding with her owner aboard works her gear as a crew does, and her owner's game does it ([together.md](m16/together.md), "What a rider can do on her"). On her rods: her spread let out and brought in, her kite on her stand-up rod, her electric reel, her chum, all with her owner's bait, and any fish on them fought on her tackle (her rod, reel, drag and line), not the guest's. At her helm, her searchlight. On the lobster boat, her deckhand: her traps set off her stack and hauled at her hauler, what's in them into the guest's own hold. Seen on everyone's game: her traps stacked on her deck (walked round), the work at her rail, anyone's chum slick (which brings up everyone's fish), and a player's flashlight. Her floodlights and underwater lights now light a guest's fish too. With it, a fix found in play: a boat taken out showed the one you'd left's hold in the bar at the top until something else changed (`App.setBoat` never told the HUD). Checked with two real games (`tools/two-players/crew.mjs`); two devices through All the Spaces still untried.

- ~~**Chum slick for guests, and deck traps for guests.**~~ Done ahead of M17 (above): both are sent and drawn.
- **A second cradle.** Marta's village has one cradle. With your boat hauled onto it, yours is what's drawn on it on your game, and a guest's boat hauled there isn't drawn (`Together`). A second cradle would give it a place.
- **The shell tray shared between players.** The tray by Marta's table is built from your own save (`shells.wall`), so each player sees only their own, where the set is meant to be laid out "for anyone to see".
- **A guest's catch going into the guest's own hold.** A 92 lb record tuna fought from the sportfisher's chair was let go because the guest's cooler holds 66 lb (seen in play). It is as designed ("the catch goes in your hold"), but it reads oddly aboard a boat with a 300 kg box. Consider the boat's hold for what a guest catches aboard her. The user, 2026-11-05: "I am good with the user using their own hold while aboard a host vessel"; so a guest's catch from her rods and her traps went in their own hold in the guest work above. Whether a big fish that won't fit should go in her box is still open.
- **A seated guest's view stays facing forward.** Seen in play with a guest on the kayak's tank well; the cause isn't traced to a line of code.
- **Spare berths.** Your own moored kayak and a visitor's copy overlap on the sand, because the spare berths along the pier's stem cover only the pier head's water (`together/Berths.js`, `atDock`), and the kayak lies on the sand. Past four spares a boat stays at her true place and may overlap another, and a boat leaving the stem's berths can pass through the pier head on your screen for a few seconds (with three or more others at the dock, on mid and high).
- **Pearl's berth with another player's boat in it.** She looks only for yours ([north.md](m16/north.md)); a guest's boat at her jetty isn't looked at.
- **A speck's height is her type's.** A sportfisher with no tower still shows past the fog as if she had one (`Specks`: `sizeOf( id ).air`).
- **A rider's horn.** A rider at someone else's helm hears their own horn from their own boat, where everyone else hears it from the boat they're driving.

- **The console's ladder on a guest's copy.** It folds down on her owner's game when she's stopped with nobody at the helm, and stays up on a copy (`ConsoleModel.dressCopy`). Sending it means another bit in the fit, and a guest's walking colliders are keyed on the fit, so it wants doing with care.
- **Boarding from the water.** A guest is told "Board Robin's boat", not "Climb the ladder", and from 4.2 m off her boarding point (`Together`), so the ladder's own reach is ignored.
- **Standing against the sportfisher's chair** reads as sitting in it on a guest's game (`Crew.js`), by how near you are.
- **A slow game in company.** A shared game's waves go by the wall clock and a boat's frames stop at 0.1 s, so under 10 fps the water moves faster than she's worked out. A console read 53 degrees hard over at 1.7 fps against 20 at any rate alone. A guest stays aboard through it.

## The lobster boat's other decks

- **A way onto her foredeck and roof.** Neither can be reached on foot. A hop in the house used to throw you out through the windshield; that's fixed, so they're only for a script. Her wheelhouse fills her beam, so there's no side deck to walk: it wants a hatch or steps of its own, and a reason to go there.

## Coming back

Reloading and leaving ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), Picking up where you left off).

- **Deck position isn't saved.** The save keeps `aboard: 'deck'` or `'helm'` and nothing about where on the deck, so reloading on the sportfisher's bridge, cabin or tower brings you back in the cockpit (`App.pickUp` calls `boardBoat`).
- **In the water far out when you leave:** you come back ashore, with her still out there. The save keeps `aboard` as `null` for someone swimming.
- **A kayak anchored while you sit in her has her anchor raised on reload.** `Game.updateAnchor` takes the anchor up when the helm is taken, and the kayak's seat is her helm, so being put back in the seat raises it.

## Looks and sounds

- **The far arms of wakes as real waves.** Every boat's wake is foam only; a real wake's far arms are smooth wave crests, which would take the sea's surface moving ([console.md](m16/console.md)).
- **Flares** are about three pale pixels on low (seen in play).
- **The speck past the fog at night** is near invisible: a boat's light reads an alpha of about 0.06 at 9 km (the formula, `exp( -3 d / LIGHT_RANGE )` with `LIGHT_RANGE` 11 km, gives 0.086 on a perfectly clear night, before the fog's fade).
- **Barn red paint** (`Fleet.PAINTS`) reads near black on her shaded side (seen in play). Dark materials in deep shade render near black, and the console's shade fill was set on white gelcoat ([console.md](m16/console.md)).
- **The underwater lights' pool** has no photograph to compare with ([sportfisher.md](m16/sportfisher.md)).
- **The turtles** don't mind the moon (a real full moon lessens what a light does to hatchlings), and what a turned-back turtle did isn't saved: come back the next morning and her tracks are the clock's ([north.md](m16/north.md)).
- **Pearl's outboard** hasn't had its level judged by ear (`PASSING` in `SoundScape.js`, [north.md](m16/north.md)).

## To watch, on a real device

- **Wake's bigger screen cover on phones.** The wakes are long now (about 170 m behind a console at 36 knots on high, 120 m on low). They are one draw, but the fill on a phone hasn't been measured.
- **A gale's draw, 14% slower on low.** The sea alone, drawn on the CPU with a phone's shader, takes 14% longer in a gale than v1.11.1's did (1,686 ms before, 1,922 now) with the gale's new look on it; a calm day is unchanged ([north.md](m16/north.md)).
- **A phone's frame rate** hasn't been read since M15's early steps (`?readout`). The pages' renders and readings are all from the CPU's GPU.
- **Two real devices through All the Spaces.** `tools/two-players/` only stands in for it ([together.md](m16/together.md)).
- **Low's 150 MB script budget.** It stays at 150 unless a real 2 GB device passes with more; only that can justify raising it. A very slow rise of script memory with company isn't ruled out (a build held between 96.6 and 99.9 MB over 16 minutes, [together.md](m16/together.md), Readings).
- **`BreakingSeas` counts only short seas.** Its figures were set on the short seas' rollers, so a long sea's head can go through a boat without coming aboard ([north.md](m16/north.md)).
- **Open, not reproduced:** a sportfisher dropped off the helm once in a sound run; a blocky catch card on low, seen with draws skipped, which may only be the skipping ([opening.md](m16/opening.md)).

## Script memory's headroom

- **Low's 150 MB is read at its peak, before the collector comes**, and the same build reads anywhere from about 120 to 150 MB run to run (M16's last checks: 136.8 and 149.1, with the shore fields at 384 cells a side on low). Live memory after a collection is about 75 to 90 MB, so the peak is mostly garbage waiting: the plants placed after the start (`Scatter`), a boat built (15 to 20 MB of working arrays) and the shore fields solved as the weather turns. Less of that garbage, or less held (the island's masks once the plants are placed, the shore field's CPU copy), would give a margin worth having.
