M20 shipped as v2.6.0 (#82). M21 takes the two things the user put first after it: every rod and reel drawn as itself, in your hands and on other players' copies; and the older sounds set right on every tier. Proposed on 2026-11-16 on v2.6.0 (`47c1351`), to be built on `m21`. It's built as M20 was:
1. This page, with the decisions.
2. Step 0: the sources.
3. Streams in worktrees, started on the user's word, merged one at a time into `m21` with a merge check each.
4. A final pass alone and with two players, and the release checks.

## The decisions (the user's, 2026-11-16)

1. **The older panned sounds set right on every tier, first** (M20's call: [M20.md](M20.md#the-calls-finish-left-and-the-users-2026-11-15)). The birds, the surf's waves, the whale, a boat heard from outside, the distant surf and the pier's lapping are off their levels:

   - on mid and high, Chromium's HRTF adds up to 5 dB to a low, even recording and takes 1 to 5 dB from a stereo one ([m20/finish.md](m20/finish.md)).

   The meter (`tools/audio/levels.mjs`) is taken to each of them, they're set to their levels, and the balance is heard and measured after.
2. **Each rod and each reel drawn as itself, live, and seen by other players.** Today three models stand for everything: the starter rod for every spinning rod and reel, the stand-up rod with its gold two-speed reel (trolling, the kite, the deep-drop, the chair), and the fly rod. Other players see the starter rod or the fly rod, whatever you hold.
   - **Rods:** the hand-me-down, the 7 ft graphite, the 9 ft surf rod, the stand-up rod and the bent-butt chair rod, each its own length, blank, grip and guides.
   - **Reels:** the old spinning reel, the smooth spinning reel, the conventional, the two-speed big-game reel, the wide 80-class and the 130-class, each on the rod in use as a real one sits: a spinning reel under it, a conventional on top.
   - **Seen by others:** your pose carries which rod and which reel are in your hands, and their game draws the same.
   - **What it mustn't cost:** built in code from shared parts, as the stand-up rod is (no download: about 1 MB is left of 80); each built the first time it's seen (nothing more before ready); the rod's own material (no new programs).

## Step 0 for the gear (2026-11-16)

Three notes, in `docs/m21/`:
- [sources-rods.md](m21/sources-rods.md): the six rods' real sizes, the boats' rod holders, the lines' colours and thicknesses;
- [sources-reels.md](m21/sources-reels.md): the eight reels' sizes, handles, weights, colours and how each sits;
- [before.md](m21/before.md): the code the stream touches, and what it mustn't cost.

Each figure is labelled: a maker's page opened, a search result only (Penn's, Daiwa's and Shimano's own shops refused the fetches), worked out from one, or judgement. What came of it:
- **The game's line figures hold up.** 868 m on the 80-class is the stated 950 yd of 80 lb; 914 m on the 130-class is its stated 1000 yd of 130 lb; 900 m on the electric reel is its stated 900 m. The other four sit inside the real reels' figures.
- **The stand-up rod is drawn too long.** Real 50 lb stand-up rods are 1.68 m (up to 1.83), their blanks 19 to 21 mm at the grip, with five rollers and a roller tip. The game's is 2.13 m, 27 mm and six rollers. Drawn as it is, it's shorter and slimmer. Its butt and gold 50-class reel match their real ones.
- **Each rod's own shape:**
  - the hand-me-down: 2.0 m of black glass with a pale clear tip, six guides;
  - the 7 ft graphite: slimmer (12 mm at the grip), a split grip, seven small guides;
  - the 9 ft surf rod: 2.74 m with a 48 cm rear grip. With the conventional reel the guides are on top and smaller, the first about half a metre ahead of the reel;
  - the chair rod: 2.35 m on a 0.72 m polished curved butt, bent about 30° (measured off one photograph: rough);
  - the fly rods: one 9 ft model per weight, its grip, fighting butt and stripping guides by weight.
- **Each reel's size:**
  - Spools are stated for five reels and worked out for the rest. The method matches every stated one to 1 mm.
  - No maker states a reel's whole size, so the bodies are measured off square-on photographs (about ±10%).
  - The reels in turn:
    - the old spinner: a 5000-size, black with a gold spool, worn;
    - the smooth one: a 4000;
    - the conventional: silver with a black lever, no harness lugs at its size;
    - the 80-class: a gold drum 150 mm across, its two harness lugs on top;
    - the 130-class: the 80's shape, 1.2 times taller;
    - the electric: a black block with a display and a ball knob, its cable to a 12 V battery;
    - the fly reels: discs of 105, 111 and 121 mm for 8, 10 and 12 weight.
- **The holders:** flush tubes 51 to 57 mm across and 23 to 28 cm deep, at 0°, 15° or 30°; a conventional reel on top of a rod standing in one, a spinning reel underneath.
- **Judgement, for the user to change if they like:**
  - **the line colours:** 8 lb mono clear; 15 lb clear with a faint green; 30 lb braid moss green; 60 lb braid hi-vis yellow (so the two braids differ); 80 lb mono smoke blue; 130 lb mono hi-vis yellow;
  - **the fly reel's handle** on the left, wound by the hand that isn't holding the rod;
  - **ring sizes** where no source gave one.
- **Not found:** a free photograph of an old Penn spinner, a fighting chair, a bent butt or a rocket launcher; any reel's whole size from its maker.

## The questions put to the user

**The gear:**
- **What else is drawn as it is:** the line's colour (clear mono, coloured braid), the fly reels by weight, the lure or bait on the hook, the rods in the boat's holders (her owner's own rods)?
- **Each shaped after a real one**, as the stand-up rod was after a Penn International 50 and a Shimano Tiagra 50W: step 0 finds the photographs and sizes for each. Shapes only, no makers' names or marks on them.
- **The pairs the game makes:** your light tackle is your best spinning rod with your best light reel, so a conventional reel can end up on the 9 ft surf rod. That's real (surf casters use conventional reels), but the rod's guides would be a casting rod's, on top. Draw what's paired, or pair what's drawn?

**The sounds:**
- **Each recording's HRTF figure** (`tools/audio/hrtf.mjs`' table, four still to measure) taken off on mid and high, or one figure for the low, even sounds and one for the rest?
- **What's measured in the game:** every panned sound, or the loudest few of each kind, with the rest set from the table?

**Found on 2026-11-16: shared sessions dropping.** Twice, about ten minutes into fishing together aboard one player's boat, each lost the other (on the user's own All the Spaces server).
- **Why it lasted:** in All the Spaces, a player whose connection to a creation's session dropped, for any reason, was taken out of it at once. The Colyseus client's own retries had nothing to come back to, and the app ignored the final leave: they played on alone till a reload, the game never told. Their connection to the space came back by itself, so the others saw them there as "Arriving…".
- **Not Salt Life's traffic:** measured with a guest fishing on the owner's boat (`tools/two-players/host.js` now meters it against the room's limits): at most 10 messages a second against 60, the biggest 384 bytes against 16 KB, the shared values 91 bytes in all. A pose goes at most ten times a second, whatever the frame rate.
- **What dropped it** is in the server's logs (`ats-admin status`, `ats-admin logs --since 3h realtime`), not read yet: the tailnet's policy doesn't let this machine onto the server.
- **Fixed in All the Spaces** (branch `session-reconnect`, not yet released or deployed): the room holds a dropped player's place 30 s for the client's own reconnection; past that, the app joins the session again with a fresh ticket and tells the game meanwhile; a creation's quality tier is picked once as it opens.
- **In Salt Life** (`Together._quiet`, `test/together-quiet.mjs`): everyone else in the session silent for 20 s is said once, "Not hearing from Sam for a while · if they're still playing, reload to rejoin them".

## How it's built

**Step 0: the sources**
- photographs and sizes of each real rod and reel type, held and in a holder;
- the four recordings `tools/audio/hrtf.mjs` didn't read on the busy machine (the hull's slap, the rod's swish, thunder, a shutter).

**The streams**
- **SOUNDS,** first: the trim and the HRTF figures, the meter on each panned sound, the balance on every tier.
- **GEAR:** the rods and reels as themselves, in your hands and on copies.
- **The deck hand** goes with GEAR (the sportfisher's copies and your pose are touched by both).

**Rules**
- No texture is added to any shader, and nothing to the download.
- Any change to a shader in the loading frame goes through the ready A/B and `tools/gpu-limits.mjs`.
- The same 16 programs before ready as v2.6.0's.
